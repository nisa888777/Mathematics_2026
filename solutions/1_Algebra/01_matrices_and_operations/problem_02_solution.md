# Exercise 2. Addition and Scalar Multiplication

$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\\\ -1 & 3 \end{pmatrix}, \quad B = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & -2 \\\\ 0 & 5 \end{pmatrix}$$

Both matrices have dimension $2 \times 2$.

---

### **1. Matrix Addition ($A + B$)**

To add two matrices, we add the corresponding entries located at the same row and column:

$$A + B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 + 4 & 2 + (-2) \\\\ -1 + 0 & 3 + 5 \end{pmatrix}$$
