# Exercise 1. Matrix Size and Entries

**Given Matrices:**

$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 & 3 \\\ 0 & 4 & 5 \end{pmatrix}, \quad B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\\ -2 & 3 \\\ 4 & 1 \end{pmatrix}
$$

---

### **1. Sizes of Matrices $A$ and $B$**

The size of a matrix is given as $(\text{number of rows}) \times (\text{number of columns})$:
* Matrix $A$ has $2$ rows and $3$ columns: **$2 \times 3$**
* Matrix $B$ has $3$ rows and $2$ columns: **$3 \times 2$**

---

### **2. Matrix Entries**

Using the index notation where $x_{ij}$ represents the entry at row $i$ and column $j$:
* $a_{12} = -1$ (Row 1, Column 2 of $A$)
* $a_{23} = 5$ (Row 2, Column 3 of $A$)
* $b_{21} = -2$ (Row 2, Column 1 of $B$)
* $b_{32} = 1$ (Row 3, Column 2 of $B$)

---

### **3. Row and Column Vectors**

* The second row of $A$ as a row vector:

$$
\mathbf{r}_2(A) = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 4 & 5 \end{pmatrix}
$$

* The first column of $B$ as a column vector:

$$
\mathbf{c}_1(B) = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\\ -2 \\\ 4 \end{pmatrix}
$$
